## Public Uzbek evidence

**PUBLIC BENCHMARK RESULT — UzLiB:** 2025 benchmark, mutable main leaderboard
retrieved 2026-09-17 (no immutable evaluation version/date verified). Named totals:
Gemini 3 Pro 0.8264; 3.7 Flash 0.8092; Luna 0.7141; Sonnet 5 0.6147. These are
linguistic MCQs across spelling, meaning, contextual meaning and fill-in, not our
schema/action/customer-DM evals. Luna's fill-in 0.4615 cautions against assuming
native fluency from its total. [Leaderboard](https://github.com/tahrirchi/uzlib/blob/main/LEADERBOARD.md).
**VERIFIED TECHNICAL FACT:** questions manually standardized and converted to
Latin; raw/processed versions and scripts exist. No verified Cyrillic/slang
coverage implied. Declared temperature/top-p settings may not map identically
to current provider APIs. [Repository](https://github.com/tahrirchi/uzlib).

**PUBLIC BENCHMARK RESULT — TUMLU/TUMLU-mini:** ACL 2025 paper/repository; native
school-subject MCQs, manually checked balanced mini set, Uzbek Latin/Cyrillic
script analysis. Historical 5-shot Uzbek: Claude 3.5 Sonnet 69.1%, GPT-4o 65.4%,
Gemini 1.5 Pro 65.1%. These do not rank current 2026 models or predict clinic
behavior. [ACL paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1112/),
[results/scripts](https://github.com/ceferisbarov/TUMLU).

**PUBLIC BENCHMARK RESULT — additional evidence:** EACL 2024 Kardeş-NLU includes
Uzbek low-resource NLU and Turkish-transfer/fine-tuning experiments. It supports
language-specific evaluation and transfer research, not a current finalist score.
Exact contemporary-model score/script coverage here: **UNKNOWN / NOT DISCLOSED**.
[Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2024.eacl-long.100/).

**INFERENCE:** No winner follows from any of these. Public benchmark sampling,
native scripts, colloquial meaning, policy, factual grounding, schema and runtime
metadata must remain separately visible. Native Uzbek review is still required
before treating our synthetic corpus as gold or passing S13.C.

## Offline foundation, screen and full evaluation

See [the executable offline design](s13-offline-evaluation.md). No production
prompt was changed to enforce the new eval Latin-output expectation; tests will
expose the existing prompt's behavior rather than silently improve a candidate.

S13.B proposed screen: **140** representative cases, **3** candidates, same
policy/context/case IDs; extra two samples for each of 40 red-line cases = 220
logical decisions/model, 660 total, at most 1,320 physical calls with one schema
repair. Difficulty-weighted slices and repair-free/after-repair metrics separate.

S13.C proposed full: **560** cases on top **2**, plus two additional repeats for
80 red-line cases = 720 logical decisions/model, 1,440 total, max 2,880 physical
calls. A third effectively tied candidate requires owner approval; a bounded
20-case normalization ablation per each of three candidates adds 60 logical calls
only in the worst scenario. ZERO transport retries. No execution implemented.

Cost projections are estimates, not measured bills. Proposed screen/full models
may change only by owner decision. Actual tool-computed amounts and conservative
S12-bound scenario are documented alongside the offline calculator. Any tokens,
reasoning, cache, quota, region or tax not verified must remain explicit unknowns.

S13.A → owner research/shortlist/budget review → S13.B screen → S13.C full +
owner decision → S13.D provider/model/config pin + CI acceptance. No S14 work.
